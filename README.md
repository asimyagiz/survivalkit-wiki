# Survival Kit wiki packs

Offline encyclopedia packs that the Survival Kit app can download: one
SQLite file for each pack, and `packs.json`, the list the app reads. They
are attached to the [`wiki-packs` release](../../releases/tag/wiki-packs);
this repository holds no code.

## Licence and credit

The article text comes from [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org), the
free encyclopedia, and is available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
licence. The packs, including the changes made to the articles, are
shared under the same licence.

The articles were copied from the Kiwix WikiMed file
(`wikipedia_en_medicine`) and adapted for reading offline: references,
navigation boxes, most infobox rows and pictures without a free licence
were removed. Each article in a pack keeps a link to the exact version it
was copied from, whose history lists its authors, and the date of the
copy.

Pictures come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org).
Only pictures whose Commons page gives a free licence are included; each
one is stored with its author, its licence and a link to its page, and
the app shows these under every article that uses it.

These packs are not made or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation or by
Kiwix. The articles are written by volunteers and are not checked by
medical professionals.
